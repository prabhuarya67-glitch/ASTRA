Google Business Profile SEO Guide

For businesses that serve customers in a defined area, Google Business Profile is an important part of local search presence. The goal is accurate, useful information—not keyword stuffing.

Keep core business information accurate

Use the real business name and keep contact details and other important information consistent with the business's actual operations.

Describe your services clearly

Choose accurate categories and describe the services you genuinely provide. Customers should be able to understand what the business does before contacting you.

Use your website as supporting evidence

Your website should clearly explain the services, service area and contact options. Link relevant service pages from your main navigation and supporting content.

Publish useful updates

Where appropriate, share genuine updates, projects, services or educational content. Avoid publishing repetitive posts solely to insert location keywords.

Encourage genuine customer feedback

Ask real customers for honest reviews and respond professionally. Do not create or purchase fake reviews.

Measure local visibility

Search Console can show how website pages perform in Google Search, while your business profile provides its own local performance information. Use both sources to understand what customers are finding.
