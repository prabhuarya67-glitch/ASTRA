SEO Checklist for Small Businesses

SEO for a small business starts with a website that search engines can crawl and customers can understand. This checklist covers the foundations before you spend time creating large amounts of content.

1. Make every important page discoverable

Your main services should have their own useful pages and should be reachable through normal internal links. Avoid creating near-duplicate pages just to target slightly different locations or keywords.

2. Use clear page titles and descriptions

Each important page should have a descriptive title and a useful meta description. The title should describe the actual page rather than repeat a list of keywords.

3. Check indexing basics

Confirm that important pages are indexable, use the correct canonical URL, and are included in your XML sitemap. Search Console can help you inspect individual URLs and understand indexing issues.

4. Improve the content itself

Answer the questions customers actually ask: what you offer, who it is for, how the process works, what information a customer needs before contacting you, and what makes your service relevant to them.

5. Strengthen local relevance

If you serve Bengaluru or another defined area, make the location clear where it is genuinely relevant. Keep business name, contact information and service details consistent across important business profiles.

6. Build useful internal links

Connect your homepage to your main service pages, and connect relevant articles back to those services. Use descriptive anchor text so visitors and search engines can understand the destination.

7. Measure before changing everything

Use Search Console to monitor impressions, clicks, queries and indexing. SEO is iterative: publish useful improvements, allow time for crawling and indexing, then evaluate the data.
